AETHELGARD: DEPTH OF STEAM

Trapped deep inside an underwater steampunk research station, you must scavenge mechanical key parts, calibrate high-pressure steam conduits, and evade the lethal Dreadnought Steam Octopus using sound stealth and sonar tracking.

SURVIVAL RULES:

• Sprinting creates loud vibrations alerting the Octopus.

• Sneaking suppresses footstep noise on sonar.

• Watch the Acoustic Sonar Radar for threat positions.

ESCAPE STEPS:

1. Find 3 key components in station chests.

2. Assemble the Gear Crank Key at the Main Hatch.

3. Balance 4 sequential steam pressure gauges.

4. Trigger the hydraulic release to escape!

Select a gear from your inventory tray and click a Pin Slot (1, 2, or 3) to mount it. Adjust the Piston Length to align with the access depth marker.
